## What is HRDCorp and who has to pay the levy?

HRDCorp is the government agency under the Ministry of Human Resources that collects a **1% training levy** from Malaysian employers and reimburses approved training costs. It was previously known as HRDF (Human Resources Development Fund) and was renamed HRDCorp in 2019.

You are required to register with HRDCorp and pay the levy if your company meets any of these thresholds:

- **Manufacturing sector:** paid-up capital of RM 2.5 million and above, OR 75 or more Malaysian employees
- **Services and other sectors:** paid-up capital of RM 500,000 and above, OR 10 or more Malaysian employees (the most common SME trigger)

The 1% levy is calculated on the monthly wages of your Malaysian employees. The money sits in a per-company HRDCorp account and is drawn down to pay for approved training — including AI training, **if the trainer and the specific course meet HRDCorp's criteria**.

## What AI training actually qualifies for HRDCorp claims?

Not every AI course is claimable. HRDCorp reimburses training only when **all three** of these conditions are met:

1. **The trainer is HRDCorp-registered.** Look for the **HRDC Accredited Trainer (HRDC AT)** credential, or verify the trainer's name on HRDCorp's official trainer directory.
2. **The course sits in a claimable category.** Most AI productivity, AI-for-business, and AI tools training falls under "Soft Skills" or "Digital Skills" sub-categories — both of which are pre-approved.
3. **The training meets HRDCorp's minimum duration and delivery rules.** A 4+ hour in-person or live-online workshop usually qualifies. Pure pre-recorded video courses typically do not.

**Before you sign anything**, ask the trainer for two pieces of evidence: their **HRDC AT number** and the **course code** in HRDCorp's registered course list. If they can't produce both, the course is not claimable.

## 5 steps to claim HRDCorp for AI training in e-TRiS

Here is the exact process, start to finish, for a typical 5-hour in-house or open-enrolment AI workshop:

**Step 1 — Confirm your HRDCorp account balance.** Log in to [e-TRiS](https://www.hrdcorp.gov.my) and check your available levy balance. The system shows the amount you have to spend on training per calendar year.

**Step 2 — Pick an HRDCorp-registered AI trainer.** Verify both the trainer (HRDC AT) and the specific course are on HRDCorp's registered list. For example, Reese Tan's 5-hour *Mastering AI for Business* workshop is a registered HRDCorp-claimable course under the Soft Skills / Digital Skills category.

**Step 3 — Lodge the training application in e-TRiS.** Submit the course title, dates, venue or online link, and the list of participants **at least 7 working days before the training starts**. Clean applications are approved in 3 to 5 working days.

**Step 4 — Run the training and keep full documentation.** Capture attendance with signatures (or, for online, the platform's join/logout logs), keep the trainer's invoice and your payment proof, and collect a Certificate of Completion for each participant. The trainer should give you all of these.

**Step 5 — File the claim within 6 months of the training date.** Submit attendance, invoice, payment proof, and the certificates in e-TRiS. HRDCorp processes clean claims in **30 to 45 working days**.

## 5 common reasons HRDCorp claims get rejected

From filing claims for SME clients, these are the five rejection reasons that come up again and again:

1. **Incomplete attendance records** — missing signatures, illegible names, or wrong dates
2. **Course not pre-approved in e-TRiS** — even fully-claimable courses must be lodged in advance
3. **Wrong claim category** — submitting under a category the course is not registered for
4. **Late submission** — filed past the 6-month window from the training date
5. **Trainer not HRDCorp-registered** — payment to an unregistered trainer is not claimable, no exceptions

A good HRDCorp-registered trainer will help you avoid all five of these. Ask before you book.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is AI training claimable under HRDCorp in 2026?

Yes. AI productivity, AI-for-business, and AI tools training delivered by an HRDCorp-registered trainer in a Soft Skills or Digital Skills sub-category is reimbursable through your company's 1% levy account. The course must be pre-approved in e-TRiS before the training date.

### How long does an HRDCorp claim take to be paid?

Clean claims — meaning complete attendance, valid invoice, and correct category — are typically processed and paid within **30 to 45 working days** of submission. Claims with missing documents or late filings can take 60+ working days or be rejected entirely.

### Do I have to use a specific trainer?

You can use any HRDCorp-registered trainer, but the trainer and the specific course must both appear on the registered list. Always ask for the trainer's **HRDC AT number** and the **course code** before booking. Both are verifiable on the HRDCorp portal.

### Can I claim for online AI training?

Yes, if the trainer is HRDCorp-registered, the course is in a claimable category, and the training meets the minimum duration. **Live, interactive** online sessions (with attendance logs) are claimable; pre-recorded video courses are usually not.

### What happens if my HRDCorp claim gets rejected?

You can resubmit within the 6-month window after fixing the rejection reason — usually a missing signature, late filing, or wrong category. A good HRDCorp-registered trainer will help you with the resubmission at no extra cost.
