## 🏛️ WHAT IS HRDCORP?

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  HRDCorp = Human Resource Development Corporation ║
║  • Malaysian gov agency under Ministry of HR      ║
║  • Collects 1% training levy from employers       ║
║  • Reimburses approved training costs             ║
║  • Renamed from HRDF in 2019                      ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

**WHO MUST REGISTER?**

| Trigger | Manufacturing | Services & others |
|---|---|---|
| Paid-up capital | RM 2.5M+ | **RM 500K+** |
| OR Malaysian employees | 75+ | **10+** ⚡ |

⚡ The **10-employee rule** is the most common SME trigger.

**Levy formula:** `1% × monthly wages of Malaysian employees` → sits in your company HRDCorp account.

## ✅ THE 3 CONDITIONS FOR A CLAIMABLE AI COURSE

```
┌─ 1. TRAINER ───────────────────────────────────┐
│  ✅ Must be HRDCorp-registered                  │
│  🔍 Look for the HRDC AT credential            │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
┌─ 2. CATEGORY ──────────────────────────────────┐
│  ✅ Course must sit in a claimable category     │
│  📂 Usually "Soft Skills" or "Digital Skills"   │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
┌─ 3. DELIVERY ──────────────────────────────────┐
│  ✅ 4+ hour duration                           │
│  🏢 In-person OR 💻 live online (with logs)    │
│  ❌ Pre-recorded video = NOT claimable          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

> 💡 **Before you sign anything** — ask the trainer for two pieces of evidence: their **HRDC AT number** and the **course code** in HRDCorp's registered course list. If they can't produce both, the course is not claimable.

## 🚀 THE 5-STEP CLAIM FLOW

```
  ┌──────────┐   ┌──────────┐   ┌──────────┐   ┌──────────┐   ┌──────────┐
  │  STEP 1  │▶▶▶│  STEP 2  │▶▶▶│  STEP 3  │▶▶▶│  STEP 4  │▶▶▶│  STEP 5  │
  │  Check   │   │   Pick   │   │  Lodge   │   │   Run    │   │   File   │
  │ balance  │   │ trainer  │   │ in e-TRiS│   │ training │   │  claim   │
  └──────────┘   └──────────┘   └──────────┘   └──────────┘   └──────────┘
      💰             🎓              📋              📸              💸
    e-TRiS         HRDC AT        ≥ 7 days        Sign-in        < 6 months
    login          verified       before           logs         to 30-45 days
                                                                    payout
```

| Step | What you do | Time it takes |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Check balance** | Log in to [e-TRiS](https://www.hrdcorp.gov.my) → see your available training fund | 5 minutes |
| **2. Pick trainer** | Verify trainer (HRDC AT) + course on the registered list. E.g. Reese Tan's 5-hour *Mastering AI for Business* is registered. | 1 day |
| **3. Lodge in e-TRiS** | Submit course title, dates, venue/link, participant list **≥ 7 working days before** training | Approval in 3–5 days |
| **4. Run + document** | Sign-in sheet (or live-online join/logout logs) + invoice + payment proof + Certificate of Completion per participant | 1 day of training |
| **5. File claim** | Submit all docs in e-TRiS within **6 months** of training date | 💸 Paid in 30–45 working days |

## ❓ FAQ — QUICK ANSWERS

**Q1. Is AI training claimable under HRDCorp in 2026?**
✅ **Yes** — if the trainer is HRDCorp-registered, the course sits in Soft Skills or Digital Skills, and it's pre-approved in e-TRiS.

**Q2. How long until I get paid?**
⏱️ **30–45 working days** for clean claims. 60+ days if anything is missing or wrong.

**Q3. Do I have to use a specific trainer?**
🎓 **No** — any HRDCorp-registered trainer works. Always ask for the **HRDC AT number** and the course code before booking.

**Q4. Can I claim for online AI training?**
💻 **Yes** — live interactive online sessions with attendance logs are claimable. Pure pre-recorded video courses are usually not.

**Q5. What if my claim gets rejected?**
🔄 **Resubmit** within the 6-month window after fixing the rejection reason (usually signature, timing, or category). A good HRDCorp-registered trainer will help you for free.

**Q6. Does HRDCorp cover AI training for real estate agents?**
🏘️ **Yes** — the same HRDCorp process applies, and an LPPEH-approved course gives attending agents **5 LPPEH CPD hours** on top of the claimable fee.
