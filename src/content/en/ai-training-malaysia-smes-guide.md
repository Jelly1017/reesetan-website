## What is AI training for SMEs, in plain terms?

**AI training for SMEs** is a short, hands-on programme that teaches small-business owners and their teams to use free AI tools to replace the most time-consuming parts of marketing, research, design, and client communication. Unlike a "data science" or "prompt engineering" course, SME AI training is built around a real Monday-to-Friday workflow: write a social post, summarise a meeting, build a proposal, research a market, draft a client email.

For a Malaysian SME paying RM 5,000–RM 15,000 per employee per year in salary, even saving 5 hours a week per marketer — at a conservative RM 50/hour fully loaded cost — returns **RM 13,000 per marketer per year** in freed time. Multiply that across a 3-person marketing team, and a 5-hour training programme pays back in under 6 weeks.

That payback is the reason HRD Corp now lists AI training as a claimable category, and why LPPEH (the Malaysian real estate board) approves specific AI programmes for CPD hours.

## How to claim HRD Corp for AI training in Malaysia

If your company is registered with **HRD Corp** (formerly HRDF), AI training is a claimable cost under the **Skim Latihan Tambahan (HRD Corp Additional Training Scheme)**. The exact claim mechanism depends on your levy tier and whether you choose a public workshop or an in-house programme, but the standard flow is:

1. **Choose an HRDC-accredited trainer.** Verify the trainer's name against the HRD Corp trainer directory at [hrdcorp.gov.my](https://hrdcorp.gov.my) before booking. Reese Tan is a registered HRDC Accredited Trainer (programme code on file).
2. **Pick a claimable programme.** The training must be on an approved scheme. *Mastering AI: Smart Prompts, Sharp Writing, Solid Research* is HRD Corp Claimable, LPPEH Approved (5 CPD Hours).
3. **Submit the training claim via the HRD Corp e-TRiS portal at least 7 working days before the session.** Late submissions are a common reason claims are rejected.
4. **Attend and document.** Get the certificate of attendance, attendance list, and training materials — HRD Corp may audit within 24 months.
5. **File the claim within the window.** Most schemes require claim submission within 30 days of the training end date.

For companies **not** registered with HRD Corp, the training can still be paid out of operating budget at a typical fee of RM 399–RM 559 per participant for a public workshop, or quoted per-session for in-house programmes (minimum 10 pax).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does AI training cost in Malaysia for a small business?

Public workshops for individuals typically run **RM 399–RM 559 per participant** for a 5-hour HRD Corp claimable programme. In-house corporate training (10+ pax) is quoted per session and typically lands in the **RM 4,000–RM 8,000** range for a half-day, fully tailored session. Companies registered with HRD Corp can recover most or all of the cost through their training levy.

### Can a non-technical SME owner really learn AI in 5 hours?

Yes — provided the format is hands-on, not lecture-based. The key is that participants build real artefacts from their own work during the workshop (a prompt library, a Canva template, an AI assistant) rather than taking notes on theory. In Reese Tan's *Mastering AI* cohorts, **100% of participants with no prior AI experience complete the workshop with at least one working artefact they use the following week**.

### Do I need to pay for ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, or other AI subscriptions to start?

No. The 5-hour SME programme is built around **free tiers only** (Gemini, NotebookLM, Canva Free, Gamma Free, Perplexity Free). The only paid tool a typical SME adds later is a domain-specific assistant or a higher Gemini tier once the team has a clear workflow — not before.

### How do I know if my company is registered with HRD Corp?

Ask your HR or finance lead. Companies that contribute to the Human Resource Development Fund are automatically registered. You can also verify by logging into the [HRD Corp e-TRiS portal](https://etris.hrdcorp.gov.my) with your company credentials. If you're not registered, you can still attend — you just pay the training fee out of operating budget.

### Will AI replace my marketing team?

No — and the workshops are built around this premise. AI replaces **tasks**, not roles. A 5-person marketing team that uses AI well produces the output of a 7–8 person team, but the team is still needed for strategy, client relationships, creative judgement, and quality control. The goal of the training is to give your existing team a multiplier, not to set up a redundancy conversation.
